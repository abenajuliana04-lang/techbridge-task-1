What TechBridge is

TechBridge is a practical training initiative from Baselink Services Limited, built for people who already know the theory and want the experience of doing real, applied work. We connect learners with structured internships that mirror how tech teams actually operate.

It's for university students, recent graduates, career switchers, and self-taught learners who are ready to move past tutorials and build something with guidance and accountability.

Who it's for
Learners ready to apply what they know to real, guided work.
What you get
A structured, time-boxed internship with practical, project-based tasks.
How you start
Apply through our form — no prior internship experience required.

30-Day Practical Internship

Thirty days of real, hands-on work.

Every TechBridge internship runs for 30 days and is built around practical, project-based tasks rather than passive lessons. You work through assignments similar to what you'd meet on an early tech role, with guidance along the way.

  • Project-based tasks

    Assignments modeled on real work, not classroom exercises.

  • Recommendation letter

    Successful participants may receive a recommendation letter based on their performance.

  • A structured 30 days

    A clear, time-boxed program — enough time to build something real.
