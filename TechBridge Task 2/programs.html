Which one is for you?

Same 30-day structure, two different focuses.

Data Analytics

Working with data and extracting insights.

You're drawn to numbers and "why." You want to clean messy spreadsheets, write SQL, and explain what the data means for a business decision.

Web Development

Designing and building websites and web applications.

You're drawn to building and "how it looks and works." You want to structure pages, style interfaces, and ship projects with Git.
