THE BIG PICTURE

Eight tasks. One bigger journey.

TechBridge is designed as a progression, not a collection of random assignments.

You begin with the fundamentals of web development, gradually take on more complex interfaces, and eventually combine your work into a complete internship platform.

YOUR SKILL EVOLUTION

Start simple. Build bigger.

The difficulty grows. So does your confidence.
